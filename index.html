International Business (22:553:593) — AI Research Validation & Citation Auditing in Global Business. Framework based on Jonathan M. Torres (2024) & Rutgers Library Studio.

Assignment reference — purpose, workflow, demo case study & rubric
Pedagogical Purpose Generative AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Gemini) produce plausible, highly structured business summaries, but frequently suffer from currency blindspots, oversimplifications, and unverified assertions. In this exercise, you will practice Socratic citation auditing by prompting an AI tool for a corporate or macroeconomic analysis (such as a SWOT or Trade Policy review), and cross-examining every claim against premium Rutgers library databases (Business Source Premier and Dow Jones Factiva).

1. The 5-Step Library Studio Workflow

  1. Source Set Builder (Grounding). Construct a closed-loop NotebookLM source set using strictly verified course readings (e.g., David Moss's Concise Guide to Macroeconomics, HBS Case Studies, or instructor PDFs).
  2. Copyright Check (Data Boundaries). Run your uploaded documents through the Rutgers Copyright Filter: (a) Public Domain/CC → Upload freely; (b) Fair Use (Excerpts) → Document "Copyright Reasoning"; (c) Proprietary Databases (HBS Cases, HBR) → STOP. Do not upload.
  3. AI Draft Generation. Prompt Gemini or ChatGPT to generate an initial structural draft (e.g., a SWOT analysis or Macro Impact Summary) for your assigned case study.
  4. Citation Audit (Database Cross-Examination). Cross-examine each segment of the AI output using Business Source Premier and Dow Jones Factiva to verify accuracy.
  5. Disclosure Note Submission. Complete your Canvas Reaction post by submitting the revised analysis alongside a Disclosure Note logging tools, prompts, database verification paths, and what changed in your argument.

2. Demonstration Case Study: Canoo Inc. SWOT Audit (Torres, 2024)

A worked example of how the chart below is meant to be used. When ChatGPT and Gemini were prompted for a SWOT analysis of electric vehicle maker Canoo Inc., both models produced realistic summaries — but library database verification revealed critical gaps.

SWOT ElementAI Generated Claim (ChatGPT / Gemini)Library Database Verification (Factiva / BSP)Outdated?Justified?
StrengthsExperienced Management Team: industry veterans from BMW, Faraday Future, and Apple.Fortune (Mathews, 2023): EV company Canoo loses 8 top executives amid severe 2022 production crises.YESNO
WeaknessesLimited Production & Battery Costs: lacks scale; struggles with raw battery costs.Reuters (Klayman, 2022) & Bloomberg (O'Kane, 2022): confirmed production delays and battery fires.NOYES
OpportunitiesGrowing EV Market & Fleet Expansion: capitalize on delivery van market.MarketLine (2023) & Chain Store Age (2022): US EV market forecast to reach $74.9B; Walmart fleet deal confirmed.NOYES
ThreatsIntense Competition & Trade Wars: rivalry from Tesla, Ford; supply chain trade risks.Business Insider (Lopez, 2023): validated China–US tech cold war and raw material lithium shortages.NOYES

Key Takeaway: While 75% of the AI's points were accurate, 25% (the Strengths) was fundamentally outdated. Without library database verification, students would submit factually flawed strategic analyses.
